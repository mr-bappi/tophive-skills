A NOTE FOR THE GROWN-UPS

Room to wonder.
Space to grow.

Little Fern starts with a simple idea: science belongs in muddy gardens, moonlit windows, and everyday conversations.

☘Made for curious minds.
Better explored together.
What will my child actually do?+

Explore three short illustrated lessons, answer a question, and try a hands-on water experiment. There are no timers or leaderboards — just room to think and try again.

Will we need special equipment?+

No science kit needed. The walking water activity uses clear jars, water, paper towels, and food coloring. A grown-up should help with the setup and supervise throughout.

Are you collecting my child’s information?+

This demonstration has no accounts, ads, trackers, or sign-up forms. Saved discoveries stay in this browser only. Please don’t enter personal information; none is needed to explore.

Is this a full learning program?+

This is a fictional platform demonstration with three sample lessons and one experiment. It is not an assessed curriculum or a replacement for adult guidance.
