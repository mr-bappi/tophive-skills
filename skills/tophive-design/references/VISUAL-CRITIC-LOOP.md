# Visual Critic & Repair Loop — render evidence, not self-praise

Used after **new website BUILD** and material redesign, with COMPOSITION-ENGINE and DISTINCTIVENESS-GATE. This is an internal iterative process within the user-authorized build, not a claim that the model can objectively grade its own aesthetics.

## Stage A — Pre-render creative veto

Before writing detailed CSS compare proposed architecture against:
- Product truth and unique evidence.
- Interchangeability (logo/font/color swap).
- Repeated three-card rows and repeated label → headline → body sections.
- Product-specific interaction: meaningful state or a deliberately exceptional reading/product experience.
- Real mobile transformation, type-scale viability and asset fidelity.

If 2+ structural decisions are generic conveniences, revisit architecture; do not merely add gradients, motion or italics.

## Stage B — Render checkpoints

At minimum inspect 390px and 1440px; 360, 768, 1024 and 1920 when available. Collect full-page captures AND close-up hero/product evidence. Record: focal point, hierarchy, dead space, scan paths, visual repetition, label legibility, demo readability, section edge transitions, actual functional interactions, focus and motion states. Compare from rendered artifacts, not source code alone.

## Stage B2 — Challenge the first rendered design

The first rendered screenshot is **evidence to inspect**, not automatic approval. At full-page scale and in a readable close-up, check the **creative outcome**, not just layout correctness:
- **Identity without logo:** Does the content and visual system convey a recognizable subject and point of view, or could another brand replace it unchanged?
- **Compositional development:** Do sections progress in scale, texture, image role and information job, or repeat headline + illustration/cards + CTA at every stop? Deliberate publication rhythm is allowed when it aids scanning.
- **Typography intelligence:** Are display, secondary, body and utility roles truly legible and varied as needed, or does one oversized headline + italic accent formula repeat without purpose?
- **Asset direction:** Are image subjects, crops and representations adequate and meaningfully distinct? Repeated artwork is allowed for a story reason, never merely because assets are scarce; avoid disguising low-fidelity or invented visuals.
- **Depth and destination:** Are there working reading/exploration paths and authentic proof, or only a handsome long page with inert controls? Would the best rejected creative candidate communicate one major idea more clearly?
- **Craft under constraint:** Does the strongest visual moment survive mobile, fallback fonts, long real content, reduced motion and usable contrast?

Name the **single largest observed opportunity** and compare a targeted structural repair with leaving the page as is. Revise when evidence indicates meaningful improvement; if the design already serves the brief and passes checks, do not force a cosmetic iteration. Screenshots, actual interaction and source must support any PASS claim; do not invent a before/after or independent score.

## Stage C — Fail-closed critique

Classify defects:
- **Blocker**: broken responsive layout, unreadable content, nonfunctional main interaction, missing primary proof, misleading feature/data, inaccessible critical control.
- **Structural**: undifferentiated section silhouette, decoration substituting mechanism, content order disconnected from visitor decisions, all sections the same composition.
- **Craft**: optical alignment, spacing, image crop, type scale, microinteraction feedback, transitions.

A structural failure cannot be 'repaired' by changing colors or radius; rework layout or content representation. A desktop-only fix cannot close a mobile failure.

## Stage D — Real revision

Choose the highest-impact root cause, change one or more actual structural axes, rerender at failed and nearby widths and document before/after. Keep approved existing brands stable; do not force eccentric novelty.

Repeat the inspection loop until blocking issues are resolved or tooling/time limits genuinely prevent completion. Report untested checks and blockers. Never say 'perfect', '9/10' or 'passed' without recorded inspection evidence.

## Portable evidence gate

The bundled scripts/visual-critic.py can generate a review template and validate completeness, screenshot signatures and before/after evidence when a structural repair is claimed. It deliberately CANNOT see whether a composition is good, nor replace a qualified independent reviewer. Its pass means 'review record complete', NOT 'creative work is excellent'.

Suggested handoff: tested viewports, most consequential critique, changed structure, interaction observed, remaining NOT TESTED and preview/artifact. Claim creative excellence only after independent visual review.
